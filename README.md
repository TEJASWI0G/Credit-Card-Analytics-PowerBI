# Credit Card Analytics using Power BI

## About the Project

This project explores credit card transactions and customer data using Power BI. I created two interactive dashboards to understand revenue performance, transaction patterns, spending behaviour, and customer segments.

The aim was to look beyond overall figures and understand how revenue varies across card categories, transaction methods, customer occupations, income groups, and other customer characteristics.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Key Findings

### 1. Credit Card Transaction Analysis

The transaction dashboard provides an overview of revenue, transaction amounts, interest earned, and transaction volume.

- **Overall performance:** The dashboard reports approximately 55.32M in revenue, 45M in transaction amount, 8M in interest earned, and 656K transactions.
- **Card category:** Blue is the leading card category, contributing approximately 46M in revenue. Silver contributes around 6M, while Gold and Platinum contribute considerably less.
- **Transaction methods:** Swipe transactions generate approximately 35M in revenue, followed by chip transactions at 17M and online transactions at 3M. Swipe transactions are the largest contributor among the three methods.
- **Quarterly trends:** Q3 records the highest revenue, at approximately 14.2M, and the highest transaction volume, at around 166.6K. Both revenue and transaction volume decline in Q4.
- **Spending patterns:** Bills contribute approximately 14M in revenue, making them the leading expenditure category, followed by entertainment at 10M.
- **Education:** Graduate customers contribute around 22M in revenue, substantially more than the other education groups displayed.
- **Occupation:** Businessmen contribute approximately 17M in revenue, followed by white-collar customers at 10M.

These findings highlight the card categories, transaction methods, and spending segments that contribute most to the revenue shown in the report.

### 2. Credit Card Customer Analysis

The customer dashboard explores revenue across customer demographics and financial characteristics.

- **Gender:** Male customers account for approximately 30M in revenue, compared with 25M for female customers.
- **Income groups:** High-income customers contribute around 29M in total revenue, combining the two series shown in the chart. Medium-income customers contribute approximately 16M, while low-income customers contribute around 11M.
- **Age groups:** Customers aged 40–50 contribute approximately 25M across the two series displayed, making this the largest age group by the combined values shown. The 50–60 age group follows at around 19M.
- **Dependents:** Customers with two dependents contribute approximately 15M across the two series shown, while those with three dependents contribute around 16M. This suggests that customers with different family responsibilities represent meaningful segments for further analysis.
- **Personal loans:** Customers with personal loans account for approximately 3.6M in revenue, compared with 3.4M for customers without personal loans.
- **State-level performance:** Texas and New York show approximately 13M in combined values each across the two series displayed, followed by California at around 13M. Florida contributes approximately 10M, while New Jersey has a lower combined value.
- **Customer occupation:** The detailed table shows that businessmen contribute approximately 17.39M in revenue, while white-collar customers contribute approximately 10.11M.

The customer dashboard helps compare different segments and identify areas where further analysis could explain differences in revenue contribution.

## Business Takeaways

The dashboards highlight several areas worth exploring further:

- Investigate why the Blue card category contributes significantly more revenue than the other card categories.
- Examine whether swipe transactions generate higher revenue because of transaction frequency, average transaction value, or differences in customer behaviour.
- Understand the factors behind the Q3 peak and the decline in revenue and transaction volume during Q4.
- Explore the spending patterns of high-income customers and customers aged 40–50 to understand their contribution to overall performance.
- Compare state-level performance to identify regional differences in customer activity and revenue.

These are potential areas for further investigation rather than proven explanations for the observed patterns.

## Dashboard Overview

### Credit Card Transaction Report

This dashboard focuses on revenue, transaction amount, interest earned, transaction count, quarterly performance, card categories, transaction methods, expenditure types, education, and occupation.

### Credit Card Customer Report

This dashboard focuses on customer revenue, income groups, gender, age, dependents, personal loan status, state-level performance, and occupation.

Both dashboards include interactive filters for exploring the data across different dates, quarters, card categories, and transaction methods.
